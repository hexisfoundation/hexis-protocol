HEXIS

A Decentralized Protocol for Trust Verification

"The people who decide have no skin in the game.
The people with skin in the game have no voice.
This protocol gives them one."

Bitcoin removed the bank.
Ethereum removed the lawyer.
Hexis removes the judge.

The Hormuz crisis of 2026 demonstrated that every centralized arbiter of trust — DFC, Lloyd's of London, SWIFT, OPEC — can be captured, weaponized, or destroyed. Hexis proposes a protocol where truth about human behavior is verified without any single arbiter.

The base unit is 1 hexis — one verifiable instance of trustworthy behavior under costly circumstances, witnessed by an independent community, recorded immutably.
